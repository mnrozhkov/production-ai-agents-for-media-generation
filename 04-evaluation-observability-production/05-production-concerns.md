# 4.5 Production Concerns and the Capstone Brief

In this lesson we take the production concerns the earlier lessons left open and turn each into a stated control in the capstone brief: auth, quotas, abuse, prompt injection through tools, content safety, licensing and provenance. No claim without a stated control. You're done when your capstone brief has one control per concern, a licence note for every model you use and a provenance statement per asset, and `secrets_scan.json` shows no secret in your traces or stored results.
